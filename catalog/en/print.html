Platform
CLS (Cord Lift System)
CLS is a system that modularizes cord winding (spool) and motor torque transmission (drive).
With various headrail/bracket configurations, it improves productivity and installation convenience and enables easy lineup expansion.

GLOBEE (Top&Down) System is a newly developed in-house brand honeycomb blind system by GLOBIS KOREA CORP.
With Top & Down operation, it provides both light control and privacy control.
It is designed to be compatible with the modular CLS component platform.
We offer the Top&Down honeycomb system under the GLOBEE lineup.
It delivers both flexible light/privacy control and a premium finish.
